# 0.8.2 and after: owner notes from the 0.8.1 RC Alpha (2026-11-03)

**Status: accepted 2026-11-03: everything below goes into 0.8.2 (owner).** The owner's first round of notes after 0.8.1, grouped by size. Built in the order A, B, C; each part tested on both e2e drivers before the next. Sizes are rough: **S** about a day or less, **M** a few days, **L** a week or more, **XL** a redesign of a whole tool. Reference apps (Arc Studio, Story Architect, Final Draft 13, Google Docs) are for ideas only: Kolbítar builds its own design and never copies their code, art or trade dress (AGENTS.md).

## Answers to the questions in the notes

- **Element autocomplete:** yes, built in M1 (`app/src/complete.ts`): character cues (from the script and the bible, most used first), scene heading prefixes, known locations, times of day and transitions.
- **PDF import:** no. Import reads Fountain, Final Draft (FDX) and plain text. PDF import needs a PDF text-reading crate (owner approval) and rules to recover screenplay elements from page positions; see item 30.
- **Tag colours:** no; breakdown tags have no colour yet (item 12).
- **Command palette:** a search box (Ctrl+K) over menu commands, scenes and characters. Two defects confirmed: choosing a character only shows a message, and choosing a scene does not move the caret (item 5).

## A. Quick fixes and wording (S)

1. **Software Tour unreadable in dark themes.** Its text colours are fixed for a light background.
2. **Remove Help > Typing Latency…** from the public menu (keep it reachable for tests).
3. **High Contrast moves into the Themes list** instead of sitting alone under Appearance.
4. **Tools become their own top-level menu** (now under View > Tools).
5. **Command palette becomes Go To.** The menu bar gets a Go To menu (page, scene, character, bookmark, and the search box); fix the scene jump; a character goes to its first speech.
6. **Renames across the app:** Comments → **Feedback**; Evidence Board → **Cork Board**; Board → **Notecard View**; Notes → **Thoughts**. Menus, titles, tour, help text, e2e, docs. File formats and IDs keep their internal names.
7. **Island toolbar:** add **Overview** and **StoryScry** buttons; fold bold, italic, underline, text colour and highlight into a drop-down under the screen-font icon.
8. **StoryScry reports get a Back button** to the dashboard instead of only Close.
9. **Home gets an Import button** beside "Open a project file…".
10. **About: pronunciation and meaning** of Kolbítar (Old Norse–Icelandic *kolbítr*, "coal-biter": the idle youth by the hearth who rises into a hero), and the nod to the Inklings J. R. R. Tolkien, C. S. Lewis and Nevill Coghill (translator of *The Canterbury Tales* into modern English). Owner to approve the wording.
11. **Revision sets:** every script starts with the original White set; File > Lock Pages offers to start a revision set; sets can be deleted (with confirmation) and the colour order closes up after a change.
12. **Colour-coded breakdown tags:** a colour per category, shown in the script, tag lists and reports (needs a schema change).

## B. Medium features (M)

13. **Software Tour rewrite:** each card about 75% "what this is for" and 25% "how to use it"; spotlight what is distinctive (StoryScry, the shared bible, revisions and locked pages, Cork Board, Thoughts, review packages).
14. **More pop-out windows:** Thoughts (the notes dock; scene list, board and notes already pop out, to be checked), StoryScry and Feedback.
15. **Thoughts formatting:** bullet lists and bold, italic and underline, in Thoughts only.
16. **Home right-click menu on a project:** change its picture, rename the project (separate from the script title), export, and backups (moved off the card). **Sort** by name, last modified or created, ascending or descending (created date needs to be stored).
17. **Live previews:** title page and headers and footers dialogs show the printed result beside the fields (drawn by the core's layout, not the web view).
18. **Runtime rail:** a vertical strip along the script's right side showing estimated running time and where you are in it (labelled as an estimate).
19. **Writing sprint mode:** a timer with a word or time goal, gentle alerts, and a congratulation at the end; sprint history optional.
20. **Branches, simplified:** under Tools, with its own window; plain words (no git terms), a picture of the versions, and two or three obvious actions.

## C. Large redesigns (L / XL)

21. **Feedback as inline threads (XL).** Select text, right-click, Add Feedback; the thread is tied to that phrase; threads show in the Feedback window (pops out) and in a margin beside the script when the Feedback icon is on; a **suggestion mode** for proposed edits that the writer accepts or rejects. Builds on M5's anchored comments.
22. **Cork Board as a free canvas (XL).** Place cards, notes and pictures anywhere; draw strings between them by dragging; its own toolset. Replaces the evidence board's current layout; existing boards must carry over.
23. **Notecard View redesign (XL).** Colour-coded story elements, visual outline and structure, season outlines, beats on cards; its own toolset like the Cork Board.
24. **Timeline as a scrubbable line (L).** The default view becomes a horizontal timeline you can scrub, add to and edit in place.
25. **Stripboard: vertical and compact views (M–L).** Alongside the current horizontal strips.
26. **Story-structure colours and season outlines** (part of 23).

## D. Needs a decision first

27. **Suggestion mode scope** (21). **Decided (owner, 2026-11-03):** suggestions never change the script. A simple icon at the right of the line opens a pop-up with the suggestion, note or comment; there is no accept-into-text.
28. **Cork Board migration** (22). **Decided (owner, 2026-11-03):** no boards exist yet, so the canvas replaces the old layout without a conversion step.
29. **Sprint mode** (19): word goals, time goals, or both? Any sound?
30. **PDF import:** approve a PDF reading crate (licence check), and accept that recovered element types are a best guess with an import report. **Decided (owner, 2026-11-03): `pdf-extract` approved.** The proposal was: `pdf-extract` 0.12 (MIT; reports each character with its position, size and font, which is what recovering elements from indentation needs). It brings `lopdf` 0.42 (MIT; printpdf already uses lopdf 0.31) and its crates, all MIT, Apache-2.0 or BSD-3-Clause (the encryption crates read password-free encrypted PDFs). Not chosen: `pdfium-render` (needs Google's Pdfium library shipped for each platform), `pdf` (parser only, no text layout), MuPDF bindings (AGPL), a reader written on lopdf 0.31 (no new crates, but font decoding would have to be rebuilt by hand).
31. **Notecard colours and layout** (23, 26). **Decided (owner, 2026-11-03):** named storylines, one per card, shared by the project; acts as columns, with a grid of a row per storyline across the acts.

## Cut

Owner, 2026-11-03: all of A–C goes into 0.8.2, with the 0.8.1 known issues. Linux builds are held back (owner, 2026-11-03) until the Windows and macOS versions are further along; Linux keeps building and testing in CI and from source. Open questions in D are asked when their item comes up.

- PDF import (decision 30): File > Import… (and Home's Import, and opening or dropping a `.pdf`) reads a screenplay PDF into a new project, a best guess with a report (`cirth_core::pdf_import`, `pdf-extract` 0.12.1 and its crates; LICENSES.md and notices updated). Elements come from where each line starts relative to the action margin, capitals and patterns; wrapped lines and wrapped headings join; page furniture is dropped; a speech split by (MORE)/(CONT'D) joins up; margin scene numbers are kept and locked; side-by-side speeches become dual dialogue; a first page with no scene or speech fills the title page; right-to-left text returns to reading order (Arabic may stay in its display forms; folding them back would need `unicode-normalization` as a direct dependency). Scanned, password-locked and non-PDF files are refused with a plain message; a reader failure is caught and reported, never a crash. Found on the way: the ToUnicode maps printpdf writes give characters past U+FFFF as odd-length hex, which stopped readers; our PDFs now write U+FFFD there (same length, nothing moves; only unused glyphs have such characters), and import repairs such maps in any PDF. Tests: unit tests for placement, page breaks, dual dialogue, the title page, refusals, CMap repair, reading order, and a round trip of our own PDFs (page-boundaries with and without a title page; unicode-marks); e2e on both drivers (export a fixture to PDF, import it, same kinds and words, report shown; a fake PDF refused).
- After the v0.8.2 macOS smoke test (2026-11-04; not in the v0.8.2 tag: the owner ships 0.8.2 as built, so these are its known issues and go in the next update): (1) a card added right after feedback or an alternate take was refused as stale ("STALE:0:1"), because those are planning steps stored by their own commands; the main window now takes the planning version they leave (`syncPlanning`), and the Feedback e2e test adds a card straight after a suggestion. (3) Home never stays blank: the project list request has a 6 s limit, is asked twice, then the window reloads once, then Home says so with Try again (e2e test with a silent request). The cause on macOS is not found; core panics now go to `logs/crash.log` in the data folder (local only).
